## GGN-EXP-01 proposed inventory (not approved scope)

| Module | Proposed formats | Proposed filters | Entity scope and permitted roles |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orders | Excel, CSV | Date, status, retailer, salesperson, Jain/Padam where authoritative | Proposed `platform_admin` and `field_manager`, each within their existing Admin order visibility and server-resolved tree; no entity inference from snapshotless history. |
| Retailers | Excel, CSV | Name, status, tier, assigned salesperson, segment | Proposed `platform_admin` and `field_manager` within existing Admin retailer/team scope. Mask private contact/location fields unless separately approved. |
| Ledger, payments, collections | Excel, CSV | Date, entity, status, retailer, method | Proposed `accounts` and `platform_admin` only, with explicit Jain/Padam entity filter and existing financial permissions; under-review balances labelled, no inferred allocation. |
| Sales performance and team | Excel, CSV | Period, metric, manager, salesperson | Proposed `field_manager` (`performance.view_team`) and `platform_admin`; server-resolved tree, leader excluded from team-member totals. |
| Visits and routes | Excel, CSV | Date, status, salesperson, retailer | Proposed `field_manager` (`visit.view`/`route.manage`) and `platform_admin`, reporting tree only. Precise location fields require separate privacy approval. |
| Leave, attendance, expenses | Excel, CSV | Date, status, employee, category | Proposed `field_manager` with the matching `attendance.review`/`expense.review` domain permission and `platform_admin`, reporting tree only; exclude receipts and sensitive reasons by default. |
| Targets | Excel, CSV | Period, metric, PERSONAL/TEAM, owner | Proposed `field_manager` (`route.manage`) and `platform_admin`, tree scoped; no cross-scope aggregation. |
| Catalogue and inventory | Excel, CSV | Category, publish status, SKU, warehouse, as-of time | Proposed `platform_admin` only for a broad export; preserve stock freshness and commitment context. |
| Service issues | **Excel implemented** | Status, retailer, staff, UTC from/through | Existing `issue.review` permits `field_manager` and `platform_admin` within server-resolved tree, with 5,000-row limit, formula escaping and no evidence attachments. |
| Survey responses | Excel, CSV | Survey, period, respondent type, status | Proposed `field_manager`/`platform_admin` with `survey.responses_view`, existing audience/tree scope; redact respondent identifiers where policy requires. |

PDF remains for document-shaped artifacts such as the existing recovery letter; it is not proposed as a bulk table format. For every approved module, freeze fields, date semantics, maximum rows, formula escaping, attachment behavior and download audit before coding. Existing utility downloads do not imply approval of these broader reports.
