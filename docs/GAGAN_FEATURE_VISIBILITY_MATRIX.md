Status is one of `PASS`, `BROKEN`, `MISSING_UI`, `PARTIAL`, `BLOCKED_BUSINESS_RULE`, `DEFERRED`. `PASS` requires reachable behavior, relevant write/readback, and authorization on the accepted release; none is inferred from code, tests, health, or a queue screenshot.
